
# Effective Prompting Techniques

Course: AI Fluency — Framework & Foundations  
Lesson: Effective Prompting Techniques  
Status: ✅ Completed

Effective prompting combines general clear-communication principles with AI-specific techniques. Below are six foundational prompting techniques and a short workflow for iterating prompts.

Six foundational prompting techniques
1. Give context: Be specific about what you want, why you want it, and any relevant background.
2. Show examples: Demonstrate the output style or format you're looking for.
3. Specify constraints: Define format, length, tone, or other restrictions.
4. Break complex tasks into steps: Guide the AI through multi-step reasoning.
5. Ask the AI to think first: Ask it to outline its plan or internal steps before producing the final output.
6. Define the AI's role or tone: Specify how you want it to behave (e.g., "You are a friendly editor who focuses on clarity").

The secret weapon
- Ask the AI itself to help improve your prompt. Provide a candidate prompt and ask the model to optimize it for clarity, completeness, and edge cases.

Iterative workflow
1. Draft a clear task overview and required format.
2. Provide one or two examples of desired output.
3. Add constraints and edge cases the AI should consider.
4. Ask the AI to propose improvements to the prompt.
5. Run the prompt, evaluate outputs, and refine.

Common patterns that work well
- Task overview + format specification + sample(s) + constraints + relevant context = high success rate.
