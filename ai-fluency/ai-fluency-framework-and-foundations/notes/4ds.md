# The 4Ds of AI Fluency

This lesson introduces the four core competencies of AI Fluency, also called the "4Ds": Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence.

Summary
- Delegation: Thoughtfully deciding what work to do with AI vs. doing yourself.
- Description: Communicating clearly with AI systems so they understand intent, constraints, and format.
- Discernment: Evaluating AI outputs and behavior with a critical eye — checking for factual accuracy, harmful content, and unexpected behavior.
- Diligence: Ensuring you interact with AI responsibly — verifying results, protecting sensitive data, and monitoring for bias or safety issues.

How the 4Ds work together
These competencies are complementary. Good Description and Delegation reduce the load on Discernment by making outputs easier to evaluate, while Diligence is the safety net that ensures your work with AI stays aligned with ethical and practical constraints.

Ways of engaging with AI
- Automation: AI executes specific tasks — Delegation is deciding which tasks are safe to automate.
- Augmentation: You and AI jointly create or decide — Description and Discernment become central.
- Agency: AI acts on your behalf — Diligence and clear long-term Description are critical.

Practical activities
- Practice deciding which tasks to delegate (small experiments).
- Write clear descriptions for a task you normally do and compare outputs.
- Assess several AI outputs for accuracy and safety (discernment checklist).
- Build a short diligence checklist you can apply before trusting an output.
