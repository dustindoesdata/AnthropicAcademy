
# AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations

Course: AI Fluency — Framework & Foundations  
Track: AI Fluency  
Status: ✅ Completed

This course introduces the core ideas and practical skills for AI Fluency. It covers the four core competencies (the "4Ds") — Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence — and shows how they apply across three ways of working with AI: Automation, Augmentation, and Agency.

Why this course
- Understand how to work effectively and responsibly with AI.
- Learn practical prompting and collaboration patterns that produce better results.
- Build a personal AI fluency plan you can use and iterate on.

Core concepts (the 4Ds)
- Delegation — Thoughtfully deciding what to give to AI vs. doing yourself.
- Description — Communicating clearly with AI systems so they understand the task and context.
- Discernment — Evaluating AI outputs and behavior with a critical eye.
- Diligence — Ensuring your interactions with AI are responsible, safe, and reliable.

Ways of engaging with AI
- Automation — AI executes well-defined tasks according to instructions.
- Augmentation — You and AI collaborate as partners for thinking and producing work.
- Agency — You guide AI to act independently on your behalf by shaping its knowledge and behavior.

Key takeaways
- AI Fluency is about being effective, efficient, ethical, and safe when working with AI.
- The 4Ds apply across Automation, Augmentation, and Agency — developing them prepares you for evolving AI capabilities.
- Effective prompting is iterative: give context, show examples, specify constraints, break complex tasks into steps, and ask the AI to help improve your prompt.
